02 · Typography

IBM Plex, three voices

Plex Sans Condensed for display. Plex Serif for body, full-ink, no softening. Plex Mono for metadata, eyebrows, code, recordation stamps. The serif anchors the editorial register and refuses the default 'developer-who-picked-Inter' read.

  • Body sets in Plex Serif, not Plex Sans. The serif anchors the editorial register and resists the 'Inter generic' read.
  • Plex Mono is never used for body copy. Only metadata, eyebrows, code, recordation stamps.
  • Headings tight: line-height 1.05, letter-spacing -0.01em.

  • No em dashes in copy. Use commas, colons, semicolons, periods, parentheses.
  • No exclamation points outside of quoted speech.
  • Italic accent is one word per heading at most; usually zero.
03 · Voice

Considered. Exact. Dry.

The voice of a senior engineer writing for peers who can tell when a claim is honest and when it is performance. Quietly funny when it earns it; never theatrical.

considered exact dry specific first-person, not memoirish comfortable with technical detail breathless performative buzzword-bingo marketing-cheerful advice-columnist
Do

I built and maintain the commerce platform that processes the orders. The interesting parts are queue durability and fraud-signal handling at the edge.

Names the work. Names what is interesting. No ornament, no exaggeration.

Don't

I'm a passionate full-stack developer leveraging cutting-edge edge technologies to build delightful, scalable user experiences.

Five buzzwords in one sentence. No specific system named. Could describe ten thousand other resumes.

Do

Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, Vectorize. Rate limit at the edge, fall back to KV when the index is unreachable.

Specific named systems. Failure modes acknowledged. Reads as someone who has run this in production.

Don't

Welcome! I'm Blake, and I love building things on the web!

Performance, not statement. Exclamation points, no information.

Do

Try the chatbot. It runs on the same edge as the site, against a 768-dimensional vector index of this resume.

Concrete. Invitation to verify. Numbers because they are real, not because they sound impressive.

Don't

Crafted with love using best-in-class tools to ship faster.

Marketing boilerplate. Each phrase is a banned adjective. Says nothing.

  • No marketing adjectives: artisanal, crafted, premium, curated, beloved, cozy, warm, inviting, thoughtfully, delightfully, lovingly, uncompromising, bespoke, leveraging, passionate.
  • No exclamation points outside of quoted speech.
  • No em dashes; commas, colons, semicolons, periods, parentheses are sufficient.
  • Every claim names the system, the constraint, or the number that backs it up.
  • First person singular when speaking as Blake; never the editorial 'we'.
  • When something is uncertain, say so plainly. Hedge once, not three times.
  • When something works, state it. Do not garnish.
04 · Tone adaptation

Same voice, different tone by surface

Listings and metadata clip down to the facts. Project case studies open with one sentence of framing and then go technical. Essays carry the dry humor at full strength but never tip into showmanship.

To be specific

Replace category words with names. 'A queue' becomes 'a Cloudflare Queue with a 30-second visibility window'. Specificity is the dominant rhetorical move.

To verify

Where possible, link to the running thing: the deployed worker, the GitHub diff, the OpenAPI doc, the talk recording. Verifiability is the brand's claim about itself.

To respect the reader's time

If a section can be 80 words instead of 250, it is 80 words. The reader is a peer engineer; they don't need ramp-up paragraphs.

Clever, sparingly

Page titles and the colophon may carry dry humor: a real-estate-listing pun, a one-line aside in the footer. Once per page at most.

Clear, always

Project descriptions, technical claims, navigation labels, and CTAs are always clear over clever. If a sentence has to be re-read to understand what's being said, it gets rewritten.

07 · Photography

Documentary, factual, rare

Photography is rare and only used where it performs work. Real subjects, natural or honest studio light, captioned in Plex Mono.

Rules

  • Real subjects only. No stock, no AI-generated decoration, no abstract gradients.
  • Natural light or honest studio light. No filter packs.
  • Frame tight on the subject. Negative space belongs to the typography, not the photo.
  • Captions always present, set in IBM Plex Mono, smaller than body, flush-left under the figure.
  • When color photography is used, dominant temperature should not fight the page's cool ground; desaturate or grade slightly cool when in doubt.

Never

  • Autoplay video.
  • People-laughing-at-laptops stock.
  • AI-generated illustration as a substitute for missing photography.
  • Drop shadows on figures.
  • Image carousels.
08 · Content pillars

What this site is about

Four pillars. Anything that doesn't fit one of them probably shouldn't be on the site.

Cloudflare-platform commerce

Building and operating commerce systems on Workers, Durable Objects, Queues, Vectorize, R2, KV. Architecture, durability, edge-vs-origin trade-offs, real failure modes.

Edge AI

Practical applications of LLMs and embeddings at the edge: retrieval-augmented systems, agent workflows, latency budgets, what works versus what's a demo.

Working notes

Short, specific writing about systems built or rebuilt. Less 'opinion' content, more 'here is what happened when we tried this'. Cadence is occasional, not weekly.

Record

The resume listing: roles, systems built, talks given, OSS work. Maintained as a live record, not a marketing document.

09 · Personas

Three readers, one voice

The site holds for all three because the voice is consistent. The tone shifts by surface, not by reader.

The Peer Engineer

A senior or staff engineer at a comparable company evaluating Blake for a role, an advisory engagement, or a conversation. Reads code samples first, copy second.

specificity · verifiability · good failure modes · absence of theater

Show me what broke and how you handled it.

The Hiring Manager

A team lead or director with limited time, scanning for credibility signals before deciding to make contact. Wants the role, the systems, and the impact in under sixty seconds.

clarity · named systems · real numbers · no buzzword smell

I have ten minutes. Convince me to schedule a call.

The Niche Reader

Someone interested specifically in Cloudflare-platform commerce or edge-AI, arriving from a Slack thread, a talk, or an OSS commit. Probably already aligned on taste.

technical depth · real war stories · named constraints · shareable artifacts

Did this person actually run this in production?

10 · Logo

Wordmark and signature mark

Synthesized placeholder. The wordmark sets in IBM Plex Sans Condensed 700, tracked +0.04em, with a single Cordovan square at the right edge as the brand's signature mark. Prototype phase will commit the final form.

Clear space

Equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or change letter-spacing or weight.
  • Place over photography without a solid plate behind it.
  • Recolor the Cordovan square. It is the signature accent and remains #890620.
